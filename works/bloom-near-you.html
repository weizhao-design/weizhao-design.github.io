BLOOM NEAR YOU 是一个结合 AI 鲜花识别、本地花店发现、线上购买与售后养护服务的数字平台。用户可以通过拍照识别鲜花品种，获取 AI 分析结果与对应养护技巧；也可以在线浏览附近花店，通过导航前往线下门店，或选择 1–3 天网购配送、当天外卖送达与提前预约服务。项目还支持用户针对已有鲜花问题进行搜索，并通过联网 AI 分析获得更具体的养护建议，帮助用户在购买前后都能更好地理解、选择和照护鲜花。BLOOM NEAR YOU is a digital platform combining AI flower identification, local florist discovery, online ordering and after-sales care. Users can photograph a flower to identify it and get AI care tips, browse nearby florists and navigate to a shop, or choose 1–3 day delivery, same-day delivery or advance booking. They can also search specific flower problems and get connected AI advice — helping them understand, choose and care for flowers before and after buying.
