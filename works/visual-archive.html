项目名称Project nameVISUAL ARCHIVE｜视觉设计探索合集VISUAL ARCHIVE — Visual Design Work
时间Date2019–2023

VISUAL ARCHIVE 收录了我本科阶段在视觉传达方向的设计探索，涵盖摄影、海报设计、动态海报、包装设计、IP 形象、丝网印刷与版式实验。这些项目帮助我建立了对视觉层级、叙事表达、图形系统与媒介语言的基础理解，也成为我后来转向交互设计、AI 产品体验与系统化设计的重要视觉能力基础。VISUAL ARCHIVE collects my undergraduate work in visual communication — photography, posters, motion posters, packaging, character IP, screen printing and layout experiments. It built my foundation in visual hierarchy, narrative, graphic systems and media, which later supported my move into interaction design, AI product experience and systems design.
