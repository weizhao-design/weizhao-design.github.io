项目名称Project nameCLOSETIFY｜AI 个性化穿搭助手CLOSETIFY — AI Outfit Assistant
分类Category[AIAI][交互设计Interaction design][原型Prototype]
时间Date11.2025
01研究与访谈Research & interviews我们访谈了 18–34 岁、觉得搭配麻烦的人。他们通常要花 10–15 分钟才能选好一套衣服。天气和体感、衣橱有没有条理、一件衣服能不能多搭，是被反复提到的三件事。We interviewed 18–34-year-olds who find getting dressed a chore. Picking an outfit usually took them 10–15 minutes, and three themes kept coming up: weather and comfort, how organized the closet is, and whether a piece works in many outfits.
02构思与原型Ideation & prototyping我们先列出可能的功能并比较利弊，再从手绘低保真走到中保真流程，把每日推荐、数字衣橱、灵感板和日历串进同一套导航。We listed candidate features and weighed their trade-offs, then moved from hand-drawn low-fi screens to mid-fi flows that tie daily suggestions, the digital closet, the inspiration board and the calendar into one navigation.
03可用性测试Usability testing3 位参与者各做 3 个任务。最大的问题出在“添加衣物”：只有 1/3 的人找到入口，扫描图标被当成扫文件或扫二维码。样本只有 3 人，所以结果写成人数，不换算百分比。Three participants each tried three tasks. Adding clothes was the weak spot: only 1 of 3 found the entry, and the scan icon read as a document or QR scanner. With only three people, results are shown as counts, not percentages.
04测试后的改版Changes after testing针对测试暴露的三个问题，我把添加入口换成“+”并加上文字，把推荐页按钮加大到 44pt，又给灵感板加了入口卡片。改版是我在课程结束后整理作品集时补做的，还没有复测。I addressed the three problems the test exposed: a “+” with a text label for adding clothes, 44 pt buttons on the suggestion screen, and an entry card for the inspiration board. I made these changes after the course while preparing this portfolio, and they have not been re-tested yet.
05核心功能Key features每日推荐根据天气和日程给出整套搭配；数字衣橱用拍照录入并自动打标签；灵感板收集看到的穿搭；3D 量体记录身材数据；穿搭日历用来提前规划。Daily suggestions turn weather and schedule into a full look; the digital closet adds items from photos and tags them automatically; the inspiration board saves looks you see; a 3D body scan records measurements; the outfit calendar plans ahead.
06最终设计与反思Final design & reflection三点反思：图标的含义要先验证，“扫描”对我们是录入衣物，对用户却是扫文件；二手数据要先核实来源再用；如果重做，我会先设计“为什么推荐这一套”，再做 3D 量体这类重功能。Three lessons: test what an icon means early, since “scan” meant adding clothes to us but scanning documents to users; check secondary data before using it; and if I did it again, I would design the reason behind each suggestion before heavier features like the 3D body scan.

CLOSETIFY 是一个基于 AI 的个性化穿搭助手，帮助用户根据衣橱单品、天气、日程与个人风格生成更适合当下场景的穿搭建议。项目围绕数字衣橱管理、智能推荐与日常决策体验展开，探索 AI 如何降低用户的选择成本，并将个人审美、生活场景与实用需求整合到更自然的穿搭流程中。CLOSETIFY is an AI outfit assistant that suggests looks based on the user's wardrobe, weather, schedule and personal style. Built around digital wardrobe management, smart recommendations and everyday decisions, it explores how AI can lower the cost of choosing and bring taste, context and practical needs into a more natural dressing routine.
