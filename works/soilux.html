SOILUX 是一个结合 IOT 感知、PHYSICAL COMPUTING 与环境交互的未来居家体验项目。项目通过实时感知植物状态与环境变化，探索数字系统如何以更自然、低干扰的方式融入日常生活，并建立人与空间之间更具情感感知的互动关系。SOILUX is a future home experience combining IoT sensing, physical computing and ambient interaction. By sensing plant and environmental changes in real time, it explores how digital systems can blend into everyday life in a more natural, low-disturbance way and build a more emotionally aware relationship between people and their space.
