SOILUX 是一个 3 人团队完成的 IoT 植物养护原型。设备插在花盆里，读取土壤湿度和室内温湿度，用灯光、手机页面和主屏小组件告诉用户“现在要不要浇水，以及为什么”。我负责推荐逻辑与状态设计、ESP32 传感器接线与程序、移动端与小组件 UI，以及 3D 打印与表面处理。原型已实现传感器读取、规则判断和三个触点的同步；用户验证在下一轮进行。SOILUX is an IoT plant-care prototype built by a team of three. The device sits in the pot, reads soil moisture and indoor temperature and humidity, and uses light, a phone page and a home screen widget to tell people whether to water now, and why. I worked on the recommendation logic and state design, ESP32 sensor wiring and code, the mobile and widget UI, and 3D printing and finishing. The prototype reads the sensors, applies the rules and keeps all three touchpoints in sync; user testing is the next step.
