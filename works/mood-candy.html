02探索过的方向：Recall LensAn explored direction: Recall Lens我们先设计了一个隐形眼镜形态的记忆回溯界面。但它仍是“帮人变得更好”的工具，没有触及技术背后的社会代价，于是我们决定转向。We first designed a contact-lens memory recall interface. But it remained a tool that makes people better, without touching the social cost behind the technology — so we changed direction.
03情绪补给系统The emotion supplement system在 2076 年，BCI 设备监测情绪状态，补给无人机按需送来专注、平静或社交舒适。系统看似关怀，实则把情绪的不稳定变成了一种需要被修正的故障。In 2076, BCI devices monitor emotional states and drones deliver focus, calm or social ease on demand. It looks like care, but it turns emotional instability into a malfunction to be fixed.

MOOD CANDY 是一个关于脑机接口时代的未来概念设计项目。当情绪可以被读取、量化和按需补给，人们是否还会自己面对情绪？项目从 BCI 趋势研究出发，构建了一个由无人机配送情绪补给的 2076 年城市系统，并以一段未来纪录短片呈现它崩溃的那一天。MOOD CANDY is a speculative design project set in a brain-computer-interface future. If emotions can be read, measured and supplied on demand, will people still face their own feelings? Starting from BCI trend research, we built a 2076 city where drones deliver emotional supplements, and told the story of the day it fails in a short future documentary.
