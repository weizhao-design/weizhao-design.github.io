2 PUZZLE 是一个以动物为主题的拼图小游戏，在两周内用 Unity 独立完成。项目完整搭建了标题页、选关、计时拼图与胜利面板的游戏流程，以及拖拽吸附交互和星级评分逻辑。2 PUZZLE is an animal-themed puzzle game built solo in Unity in two weeks, with a full flow from title screen and level select to timed puzzles and a win panel, plus drag-and-snap interaction and star scoring.
