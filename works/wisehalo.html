00概要Overview问题：50–65 岁的人在诈骗里最缺的，不是更多警告，而是关键时刻有人解释“为什么可疑”，并帮他们停下来。
洞察：用户想要提醒和解释；但真正止损的案例，都靠暂停和第三方核实。
方案：AI 解释风险的 iOS App + 桌上提醒器——风险越高反馈越强，高风险时红灯一直亮，直到用户暂停或核实。
我的角色：独立完成研究、交互与界面、iOS 原型和 ESP32 硬件。
20 个公开案例 · 32 份问卷 · 4 位长辈测试 · 3 级风险反馈 · 直接试玩 ↓Problem: people aged 50–65 don’t need more warnings – they need someone to explain why something is suspicious, and help them stop at the critical moment.
Insight: users asked for alerts and explanations, yet every stopped case relied on a pause and a third party.
Design: an iOS app where AI explains the risk, plus a desk reminder – stronger risk, stronger response; on high risk the red light stays on until the user pauses or verifies.
My role: solo – research, interaction & UI, iOS prototype and ESP32 hardware.
20 public cases · 32 surveys · 4 user tests · 3 risk levels · Try the demo ↓
01问题：诈骗是一个系统Problem: scams are systems公开案例显示，诈骗从社交平台和日常任务开始接触用户，用虚假身份建立信任，再用紧迫感和小额回报推动用户做出高风险操作。大多数人只看到表面信息，看不到背后的运作方式，也就很难在中途停下来。Public cases show scams reach people through everyday channels, build trust with fake identities, then use urgency and small rewards to push risky actions. Most people only see the surface message, not the system behind it — so it is hard to stop midway.
02研究：20 个公开案例 + 32 份问卷Research: 20 public cases + 32 surveys我分析了 20 个中国 50–65 岁受害者的公开案例，并对 32 人做了线上问卷（其中 50 岁以上 15 人）。大多数人在可疑互动中会提前退出，但最需要的不是更多警告，而是“为什么可疑”的具体解释、可以马上执行的下一步，以及对保护工具本身的信任。I analysed 20 public cases of victims aged 50–65 in China and ran an online survey with 32 people (15 aged 50+). Most respondents already exit suspicious interactions early — what they need is not more warnings, but a specific explanation of why something is suspicious, an actionable next step, and trust in the protective tool itself.
03策略：风险越高，反馈越强Strategy: stronger risk, stronger response设备的反馈随风险逐级升级：低风险用黄色呼吸灯提示“核实来源”；中风险用橙色双脉冲并解释诈骗手法、建议联系信任的人；高风险用红色快闪和重复震动，配合手机全屏提示暂停付款。始终由用户自己决定下一步。Feedback escalates with risk: low risk shows a yellow breathing light and suggests checking the source; medium risk adds an orange double pulse, explains the tactic and suggests contacting someone trusted; high risk uses a fast red flash and repeated vibration with a full-screen prompt to pause the payment. The user always makes the final call.
04系统与信息架构System & information architecture手机端负责接收可疑内容、调用 AI 分析并解释风险；设备端把风险等级转化为灯光与震动。信息架构围绕“检查 → 理解 → 选择安全行动”三步组织。The phone receives suspicious content, calls the AI analysis and explains the risk; the device translates the risk level into light and vibration. The information architecture follows three steps: check → understand → choose a safe action.
05界面：从中保真到高保真Interface: from mid-fi to hi-fi首页就是由 App 图标生成的“光环开关”：打开是提醒器守护中，关掉是只用手机提醒。鲜艳的颜色只留给光环和风险等级；结果页按“风险等级 → 一句话指令 → 一个主按钮 → 为什么可疑 → 求助”排列。下面依次是：中保真、设计规范、高保真精选与深色模式、引导页。Home is a “halo switch” drawn from the app icon: on means the reminder is guarding, off means phone-only alerts. Bright colour is kept for the halo and risk levels; results follow risk level → one instruction → one primary action → why it’s suspicious → get help. Below: mid-fi, design system, selected hi-fi screens with dark mode, and onboarding.
06可交互原型：试一试Interactive prototype: try it先走 4 步引导页，再试：粘贴或选一条示例 → 开始检查 → 看风险结果、先暂停 1 分钟、发给家人；不想打字可以用“通话快速自查”。首页光环可在“只用手机”和“手机 + 提醒器”之间切换。原型用固定规则代替 AI。Start with the 4-step onboarding, then paste or pick an example → Check → see the result, take a 1-minute pause or send it to family; or use the no-typing call self-check. The halo switches between phone-only and phone + reminder. Fixed rules stand in for the AI.
07硬件原型：灯光与震动语言Hardware: a language of light and vibration用 ESP32-C3、RGB 灯环和震动马达搭建原型：先在 Wokwi 中仿真三种风险等级的反馈逻辑，再在面包板上逐项测试灯光和震动，最后让 App 的 AI 分析结果驱动设备反馈。Built with an ESP32-C3, an RGB LED ring and a vibration motor: the feedback logic for three risk levels was first simulated in Wokwi, then tested on a breadboard, and finally driven by the app's AI analysis result.
08用户测试：第一轮Usability test: round 1在国内远程测试了 4 位 50–55 岁的目标用户（1 男 3 女）：7 个任务，209 次操作全部自动记录。所有人都独立完成了引导和「发短信给家人」；但只有 1 人做完了 1 分钟暂停，用自己的话描述诈骗时，5 次检查都被判成“低风险”。一位参与者问：“钱不是我花的，它却说低风险，是不是哪里不对？”每条发现都对应一项下一版的修改。「只有手机 vs 手机 + 提醒器」的对比放到第二轮测试。Remote tests in China with four target users aged 50–55 (one man, three women): seven tasks, all 209 actions logged automatically. Everyone completed onboarding and texted family on their own, but only one finished the 1-minute pause, and all five checks typed in their own words came back “low risk”. One tester asked: “The money wasn’t spent by me, but it says low risk — is something wrong?” Every finding maps to a change in the next build. Comparing phone-only with phone + reminder is left for round two.
09现状与下一步Status & next steps已实现：SwiftUI 高保真 App + ESP32 提醒器，AI 分析结果驱动灯光与震动；另有通话快速自查、读给我听、屏幕共享警告。
已知局限：iOS 不能读取通话，只能事后自查；演示版用规则代替 AI；骗子可能在电话里指导用户关掉提醒。
下一步：先完成第一轮测试得出的 6 项修改，再对 5 位 50–65 岁的用户做第二轮测试：重测新的暂停设计，对比“只有手机”和“手机 + 提醒器”。Working today: a SwiftUI hi-fi app and an ESP32 reminder, with AI results driving light and vibration; plus call self-check, read-aloud and screen-sharing warnings.
Known limits: iOS can’t read calls, so checks happen after the fact; the demo uses rules instead of AI; a scammer could coach the user to switch the reminder off.
Next: ship the six fixes from round one, then run round two with five users aged 50–65 — re-testing the new pause and comparing phone-only with phone + reminder.

WISEHALO 是我的硕士毕业设计，一个结合 AI 风险分析与实体提醒设备的防诈骗体验项目。项目把可疑信息交给 AI 解释“为什么可疑”，并通过灯光与震动在关键时刻提醒用户先停下来，探索如何让隐藏在日常沟通中的诈骗系统变得可见、可理解、可应对。WISEHALO is my MA thesis: an anti-scam experience combining AI risk analysis with a physical alert device. It asks AI to explain why a message is suspicious and uses light and vibration to prompt a pause at the critical moment — making the scam systems hidden in everyday communication visible, understandable and actionable.
