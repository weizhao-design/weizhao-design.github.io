01问题：诈骗是一个系统Problem: scams are systems公开案例显示，诈骗从社交平台和日常任务开始接触用户，用虚假身份建立信任，再用紧迫感和小额回报推动用户做出高风险操作。大多数人只看到表面信息，看不到背后的运作方式，也就很难在中途停下来。Public cases show scams reach people through everyday channels, build trust with fake identities, then use urgency and small rewards to push risky actions. Most people only see the surface message, not the system behind it — so it is hard to stop midway.
02研究：20 个公开案例 + 32 份问卷Research: 20 public cases + 32 surveys我分析了 20 个中国 50–65 岁受害者的公开案例，并对 32 人做了线上问卷（其中 50 岁以上 15 人）。大多数人在可疑互动中会提前退出，但最需要的不是更多警告，而是“为什么可疑”的具体解释、可以马上执行的下一步，以及对保护工具本身的信任。I analysed 20 public cases of victims aged 50–65 in China and ran an online survey with 32 people (15 aged 50+). Most respondents already exit suspicious interactions early — what they need is not more warnings, but a specific explanation of why something is suspicious, an actionable next step, and trust in the protective tool itself.
03策略：风险越高，反馈越强Strategy: stronger risk, stronger response设备的反馈随风险逐级升级：低风险用黄色呼吸灯提示“核实来源”；中风险用橙色双脉冲并解释诈骗手法、建议联系信任的人；高风险用红色快闪和重复震动，配合手机全屏提示暂停付款。始终由用户自己决定下一步。Feedback escalates with risk: low risk shows a yellow breathing light and suggests checking the source; medium risk adds an orange double pulse, explains the tactic and suggests contacting someone trusted; high risk uses a fast red flash and repeated vibration with a full-screen prompt to pause the payment. The user always makes the final call.
04系统与信息架构System & information architecture手机端负责接收可疑内容、调用 AI 分析并解释风险；设备端把风险等级转化为灯光与震动。信息架构围绕“检查 → 理解 → 选择安全行动”三步组织。The phone receives suspicious content, calls the AI analysis and explains the risk; the device translates the risk level into light and vibration. The information architecture follows three steps: check → understand → choose a safe action.
05界面：从中保真到高保真Interface: from mid-fi to hi-fi高保真把首页做成一个由 App 图标生成的“光环开关”：打开是提醒器守护中，关掉是只用手机提醒。主题色取自图标的藏青与蓝青，鲜艳的颜色只留给光环和风险等级；结果页按“风险等级 → 一句话指令 → 一个主按钮 → 为什么可疑 → 求助”排列，并提供深色模式。下方依次是设计规范、全部高保真界面与深色模式；最后三张是早期的中保真。The hi-fi turns Home into a “halo switch” drawn from the app icon: on means the reminder is guarding, off means phone-only alerts. Colours come from the icon’s navy and blue-cyan, with bright colour reserved for the halo and risk levels. Results follow risk level → one instruction → one primary action → why it’s suspicious → get help, with a dark mode. Below: the design system, then every hi-fi screen plus dark mode; the last three images are the earlier mid-fi.
06可交互原型：试一试Interactive prototype: try it这是远程可用性测试使用的原型：点“粘贴”或选一条示例，再点“开始检查”，可以看到风险结果、先暂停 1 分钟、三步核实、发给家人，以及不用打字的“通话快速自查”。首页的光环可以在“只用手机提醒”和“手机 + 提醒器”之间切换——这正是测试中对比的两组。原型用固定规则代替 AI，提醒器状态为模拟。This is the prototype used for remote usability testing: tap Paste or pick an example, then Check, to see the risk result, the 1-minute pause, three-step verification, sending it to family, and the no-typing call self-check. The halo on Home switches between phone-only alerts and phone + reminder — the two conditions compared in testing. The prototype uses fixed rules instead of AI, and the reminder is simulated.
07硬件原型：灯光与震动语言Hardware: a language of light and vibration用 ESP32-C3、RGB 灯环和震动马达搭建原型：先在 Wokwi 中仿真三种风险等级的反馈逻辑，再在面包板上逐项测试灯光和震动，最后让 App 的 AI 分析结果驱动设备反馈。Built with an ESP32-C3, an RGB LED ring and a vibration motor: the feedback logic for three risk levels was first simulated in Wokwi, then tested on a breadboard, and finally driven by the app's AI analysis result.
08现状与下一步Status & next steps目前已实现：高保真 iOS App（SwiftUI）——提交文字或截图 → AI 分析 → 风险等级、解释与建议 → 提醒器灯光与震动；手机负责“提醒一次”，桌上的提醒器“一直提醒，直到您暂停或核实”。另有通话快速自查、读给我听、屏幕共享警告和 App 内字号。下一步：对 5 位 50 岁以上长辈做远程可用性测试（含“只有手机 / 手机 + 提醒器”对比）和专家评估。Working today: a hi-fi iOS app (SwiftUI) — submit text or a screenshot → AI analysis → risk level, explanation and advice → light and vibration on the reminder. The phone alerts once; the desk reminder keeps alerting until you pause or verify. Also: a call self-check, read-aloud, a screen-sharing warning and in-app text size. Next: remote usability testing with five adults aged 50+ (including phone-only vs phone + reminder) and an expert evaluation.

WISEHALO 是我的硕士毕业设计，一个结合 AI 风险分析与实体提醒设备的防诈骗体验项目。项目把可疑信息交给 AI 解释“为什么可疑”，并通过灯光与震动在关键时刻提醒用户先停下来，探索如何让隐藏在日常沟通中的诈骗系统变得可见、可理解、可应对。WISEHALO is my MA thesis: an anti-scam experience combining AI risk analysis with a physical alert device. It asks AI to explain why a message is suspicious and uses light and vibration to prompt a pause at the critical moment — making the scam systems hidden in everyday communication visible, understandable and actionable.
