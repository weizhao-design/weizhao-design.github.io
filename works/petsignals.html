PETSIGNALS 是一个结合 AI 分析与情绪交互的未来宠物陪伴体验项目。项目尝试通过行为识别、情绪反馈与界面交互，探索数字系统如何帮助人类更直观地理解宠物状态，并建立更加自然、情感化的人宠互动关系。PETSIGNALS is a pet companion experience combining AI analysis with emotional interaction. Through behaviour recognition, mood feedback and interface design, it explores how digital systems can help people understand their pets more intuitively and build a more natural, emotional bond.
