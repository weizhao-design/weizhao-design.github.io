00概要Overview问题：粉丝创作者有好点子却没有合法的变现途径，粉丝买不到“懂圈子”的周边，IP 方也很难在投产前知道粉丝真正想要什么。
洞察：三方缺的是同一样东西——在生产之前就能被验证的需求信号。
方案：创作者按 IP 方开放的品类提交周边提案，先过自动预检，再由社区点赞、评论和预售意向验证需求；达标后进入 IP 方审核与授权，生产上架后创作者拿版税。
我的角色：3 人团队中负责研究与策略（市场测算、竞品、商业模式）、体验（用户流程、旅程图）、全部高保真界面与视觉；课程结束后，借助 AI 编程工具把高保真搭成可点击原型并补齐缺失页面。
3 类用户 · 5 步提案 · 8 阶段进度 · 26 个可点击页面 · 直接试玩 ↓Problem: fan creators have ideas but no legal way to sell them, fans can’t find merch that gets the fandom, and IP owners can’t tell what fans want before they produce.
Insight: all three sides lack the same thing — a demand signal that is proven before anything is made.
Design: creators propose merch in the categories an IP owner has opened, pass an automatic pre-check, and the community validates demand with picks, comments and pre-order intents; proposals that hit their goal go to the IP owner for review and licensing, and creators earn royalties once it ships.
My role: in a team of three — research and strategy (market sizing, competitors, business model), experience (user flow, journey map), all hi-fi UI and visual design; after the course I used AI coding tools to turn the hi-fi into a clickable prototype and fill in the missing screens.

01三方问题A three-sided problem粉丝创作者有创意也有受众，却没有合法、门槛低的商业化途径；粉丝想要懂圈子、做工好的周边，现有选择却多是非官方或粗制滥造；IP 方缺少低风险的方式开发粉丝真正想要的周边，非官方周边还在侵蚀授权收入。仅 2024 年，Etsy 就因知识产权问题下架了约 83.2 万件商品。Fan creators have ideas and audiences but no legal, accessible way to sell IP-inspired merch. Fans want well-made merch that gets the fandom, yet most options are unofficial or low quality. IP owners lack a low-risk way to make what fans actually want, while unofficial merch eats into licensing revenue. In 2024 alone, Etsy removed about 832,000 listings over IP issues.
02市场与竞争Market & competition按 Licensing International《2024 Global Licensing Industry Study》，2023 年全球授权商品与服务零售额约 3,565 亿美元，其中娱乐 / 角色类约 1,476 亿美元（TAM）；美国与加拿大授权市场 2,103 亿美元，按同样 41.4% 的娱乐 / 角色占比估算，SAM 约 871 亿美元（这是估算，且包含 IPLY 不做的授权服务，所以是上限）；再假设可获得 0.5% 的份额，SOM 为 4.35 亿美元交易额，按 10% 佣金约 4,350 万美元平台收入。竞品中，官方商店授权程度高但创作空间小，Etsy、Redbubble、Behance / Pinterest 创作灵活却没有授权通道，IPLY 瞄准两者兼具的空白。

自下而上校验（第 3 年、北美、假设全部列出）：40 个 IP 上线 × 每个 IP 每月约 60 个提案 × 5% 达标并获授权 ≈ 每年 1,440 款新品；每款平均卖出 400 件 × 均价 30 美元 ≈ 1,730 万美元交易额，按 10% 佣金约 173 万美元，加上 40 个 IP 方每月 500 美元订阅约 24 万美元。这只是上面 SOM 的约 4%——SOM 应该当作长期上限，而不是三年预测；最敏感的假设是「预售意向 → 实际购买」的转化率。According to Licensing International’s 2024 Global Licensing Industry Study, retail sales of licensed merchandise and services reached $356.5B worldwide in 2023, with entertainment and character IP at $147.6B (TAM). The U.S. and Canada accounted for $210.3B; applying the same 41.4% entertainment share gives an estimated SAM of $87.1B (an estimate, and an upper bound because it includes licensed services IPLY doesn’t sell). Assuming a 0.5% obtainable share puts the SOM at $435M in GMV, or about $43.5M in platform revenue at a 10% commission. Official stores are fully licensed but leave little creative room, while Etsy, Redbubble and Behance / Pinterest are creative but offer no licensing path. IPLY targets the gap that has both.

Bottom-up check (year 3, North America, every assumption stated): 40 live IPs × about 60 proposals per IP per month × 5% reaching their goal and getting licensed ≈ 1,440 new products a year; 400 units each at an average $30 ≈ $17.3M GMV, or about $1.73M at a 10% commission, plus about $0.24M from 40 IP owners on a $500/month subscription. That is only about 4% of the SOM above, so the SOM is a long-term ceiling rather than a three-year forecast. The most sensitive assumption is how many pre-order intents turn into purchases.
03机会与用户Opportunity & users问卷由团队成员执行：72% 的受访粉丝创作或分享过同人作品、设计或点子，85% 希望看到自己喜欢的同人设计变成官方周边。样本量没有记录，所以这里只作为初步信号。三个 persona 分别代表创作者、粉丝和 IP 方。In a survey run by a teammate, 72% of fans had created or shared fan art, designs or ideas, and 85% would like to see their favourite fan designs become official merch. The sample size wasn’t recorded, so treat these as preliminary signals. The three personas represent the creator, the fan and the IP owner.

Proposal flow (second figure): it started with six steps, including a separate Details step. That step felt tedious, so its content was merged into step 4, which was renamed from Audience to Audience & Price (the title, suggested price and validation goal now live there), leaving five: choose IP → product type → upload → audience & price → publish.

08商业模式与路线图Business model & roadmap收入来自三部分：交易佣金、IP 方的赞助活动与广告费，以及面向 IP 方的 B2B 订阅（需求数据与审核工具）。主要成本集中在产品研发、IP 审核与法律合规、社区运营、IP 方拓展，以及云服务、支付与客服。路线图分五个阶段：创作者提交与 IP 匹配 MVP → 社区验证系统 → IP 方审核后台 → 授权生产与销售上线 → 可扩展的 IP 周边生态。

服务蓝图（第一张图）：IP 方作为官方品牌销售，平台抽 10% 佣金；创作者按净销售额拿版税，比例由 IP 方开放项目时设定（原型里各 IP 在 6–12% 之间，图中示例按 8%）；粉丝的预售只在授权签署后才扣款，生产取消则全额退款。

法律框架：① 投稿协议——创作者保留原创部分的著作权，验证期内只授予平台展示权；② 被采纳后，创作者与 IP 方就该商品签独家授权（期限、地区、版税写进合同），衍生作品以 IP 方授权为前提；③ 未被采纳的设计归创作者，IP 方在开放项目条款中承诺 24 个月内不使用或仿制，平台保留带时间戳的提交记录作为凭证；④ Remix 必须署名原作者，上架后原作者分得一半版税；⑤ 侵权举报与下架流程放在页脚 Report Infringement。以上为概念设计，并非法律意见。

冷启动：先从没有周边团队、又最需要需求数据的独立 IP 切入（独立游戏、网络漫画、VTuber），首批 10 个 IP + 50 位受邀创作者；粉丝通过创作者自己的社交受众进来，再用成功案例去谈大 IP。
北极星指标：每月由粉丝提案并成功授权上架的商品数。配套指标：意向 → 购买转化率、从提交到 IP 方决定的天数、每月发放的创作者版税。Revenue comes from three streams: a commission on each sale, sponsored IP campaigns and advertising, and a B2B subscription that gives IP owners demand data and review tools. The main costs are product and engineering, IP review and legal compliance, community growth, IP-owner acquisition, and cloud, payment and support operations. The roadmap has five phases: a creator submission and IP-matching MVP → a community validation system → an IP owner approval portal → licensed production and sales → a scalable IP merch ecosystem.

Service blueprint (first figure): the IP owner sells as the official brand, iPly takes a 10% commission, and the creator earns a royalty on net sales at a rate the IP owner sets when opening a program (6–12% across the IPs in the prototype; 8% in the example). Fans are only charged once the license is signed, and are refunded in full if the run is cancelled.

Legal framework: ① a submission agreement — creators keep copyright in their original work and grant iPly only a display license during validation; ② once adopted, the creator and IP owner sign an exclusive license for that product (term, territory and royalty in the contract), with the derivative work resting on the IP owner’s license; ③ designs that are not adopted stay with the creator — IP owners commit in their program terms not to use or imitate them for 24 months, and iPly keeps time-stamped submission records as evidence; ④ remixes must credit the original creator, who receives half of the royalty once the remix launches; ⑤ a report-and-takedown process sits behind “Report Infringement” in the footer. This is a concept, not legal advice.

Cold start: begin with indie IPs that have no merch team and most need demand data (indie games, webcomics, VTubers) — 10 IPs and 50 invited creators at launch. Fans arrive through the creators’ own audiences, and early success stories open conversations with larger IPs.
North star metric: fan-proposed products licensed and launched per month. Supporting metrics: intent-to-purchase conversion, days from submission to an IP owner decision, and creator royalties paid each month.
09反思与下一步Reflection & next steps做对了什么：把粉丝、创作者和 IP 方的问题归结为同一个缺口——生产之前就能被验证的需求信号；“意向不扣款、授权后才收钱”把粉丝的风险降到最低。
不足：一手数据弱，问卷没有记录样本量；课程期间没有做可用性测试；Figma 稿里有统计和文案错误，IP 方后台和提案第 4–5 步原本没有设计。
课程后补了什么：借助 AI 编程工具把高保真做成可点击原型并补齐缺失页面，修正 Figma 里的数据错误，对比度改到 WCAG AA，补上用户流程、服务蓝图、法律框架和自下而上的市场估算。
最大的假设与下一步：预售意向到底有多少会变成真实购买。下一步：① 按已写好的方案做 5 + 2 人可用性测试（原型内置了测试记录模式，尚未执行）；② 做“免费意向 vs. 5 美元可退押金”的 A/B 测试；③ 重新发放问卷并公开样本量与渠道，再替换落地页上的 72% / 85%。What worked: framing the fan, creator and IP owner problems as one missing piece — a demand signal proven before production — and lowering the fan’s risk with “intent is free, you’re charged only after the license is signed.”
What fell short: weak first-hand data (the survey’s sample size wasn’t recorded), no usability testing during the course, number and copy errors in the Figma file, and no designs for the IP owner portal or proposal steps 4–5.
What I added after the course: with AI coding tools I turned the hi-fi into a clickable prototype and built the missing screens, fixed the data errors in Figma, brought contrast up to WCAG AA, and added the user flow, service blueprint, legal framework and a bottom-up market check.
Biggest assumption and next steps: how many pre-order intents turn into real purchases. Next: ① run the prepared usability test with 5 fans and creators plus 2 IP-side participants (the prototype has a built-in session log; not run yet); ② A/B test a free intent against a refundable $5 deposit; ③ re-run the survey with a recorded sample and channels before replacing the 72% / 85% on the landing page.

IPLY 是一个连接粉丝创作者与 IP 方的周边共创与授权平台。粉丝提交周边提案，由社区投票与预售验证需求，再交给 IP 方审核授权并生产上架，探索如何让粉丝创意以合规、可持续的方式变成官方商品。IPLY is a co-creation and licensing platform connecting fan creators with IP owners. Fans submit merch proposals, the community validates demand through votes and pre-orders, and IP owners approve and license them for production — exploring how fan creativity can become official products in a legitimate, sustainable way.
