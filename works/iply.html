01三方问题A three-sided problem粉丝创作者有创意也有受众，却没有合法、门槛低的商业化途径；粉丝想要懂圈子、做工好的周边，现有选择却多是非官方或粗制滥造；IP 方缺少低风险的方式开发粉丝真正想要的周边，非官方周边还在侵蚀授权收入。仅 2024 年，Etsy 就因知识产权问题下架了约 83.2 万件商品。Fan creators have ideas and audiences but no legal, accessible way to sell IP-inspired merch. Fans want well-made merch that gets the fandom, yet most options are unofficial or low quality. IP owners lack a low-risk way to make what fans actually want, while unofficial merch eats into licensing revenue. In 2024 alone, Etsy removed about 832,000 listings over IP issues.
02市场与竞争Market & competition全球授权商品与服务市场约 3,696 亿美元，其中娱乐 / 角色类约 1,498 亿美元（TAM）；聚焦北美娱乐 / 角色授权商品，SAM 约 871 亿美元；按 0.5% 的可获得份额估算，SOM 为 4.35 亿美元交易额，按 10% 佣金约 4,350 万美元平台收入。竞品中，官方商店授权程度高但创作空间小，Etsy、Redbubble、Behance / Pinterest 创作灵活却没有授权通道，IPLY 瞄准两者兼具的空白。The global licensed merchandise and services market is about $369.6B, with entertainment and character IP at $149.8B (TAM). Focusing on North American entertainment and character merch gives a SAM of about $87.1B; a 0.5% obtainable share puts the SOM at $435M in GMV, or about $43.5M in platform revenue at a 10% commission. Official stores are fully licensed but leave little creative room, while Etsy, Redbubble and Behance / Pinterest are creative but offer no licensing path. IPLY targets the gap that has both.
03机会与用户Opportunity & users问卷由团队成员执行：72% 的受访粉丝有创作或分享同人设计的经验，85% 愿意把自己的设计变成官方授权周边。样本量没有记录，所以这里只作为初步信号。三个 persona 分别代表创作者、粉丝和 IP 方。In a survey run by a teammate, 72% of fans had created or shared fan designs and 85% would turn their own designs into officially licensed merch. The sample size wasn’t recorded, so treat these as preliminary signals. The three personas represent the creator, the fan and the IP owner.
04产品策略与核心流程Strategy & core flow构思 → 提交 → 社区验证 → IP 方授权 → 生产上架，五步把粉丝创意变成官方商品。最后一张是完整的 user flow：创作者与粉丝、IP 方两条主线，以及顶部导航、头像菜单和回到首页的方式（点击图片可放大）。Ideate → submit → community validation → IP approval → production: five steps from fan idea to official product. The last figure maps the full user flow — the creator and fan path, the IP owner path, and the header, avatar menu and ways back to the home page (click to enlarge).
05高保真原型High-fidelity prototype高保真围绕创作者的主流程：注册 → 选择 IP → 选择品类并上传设计 → 在 Ideas 页接受社区的投票、收藏与评论 → 在 My Proposals 中追踪提案从草稿、预检、社区验证到授权上架的每一步状态。另外为 IP 方单独设计了落地页，主打「先看到粉丝需求，再决定授权什么」。最后四页是视觉设计：Logo、色彩与字体、UI 组件，以及商品卡片。The hi-fi follows the creator’s main path: sign up → choose an IP → pick a product type and upload the design → gather votes, saves and comments on the Ideas page → track each proposal in My Proposals, from draft and pre-check through community validation to licensing and launch. A separate landing page for IP owners leads with fan demand: see what fans want before deciding what to license. The last four boards cover the visual design: logo, colour and typography, UI components, and the product card.
06可交互原型：试一试Interactive prototype: try it根据 Figma 高保真搭建的可点击网站：注册三步 → 浏览 Ideas、选颜色、加入预售、评论 → 五步创建提案并通过自动预检 → 在 My Proposals 追踪进度 → 切到 IP 方视角批准提案，创作者那边的状态会同步变成 Authorized。Figma 里缺的第 4、5 步、预检界面、IP 方审核后台等都已补齐。数据只保存在本地浏览器。A clickable site built from the Figma hi-fi: sign up in three steps → browse Ideas, switch colours, join a pre-order, comment → build a proposal in five steps with an automatic pre-check → track it in My Proposals → switch to the IP owner view and approve it, and the creator’s proposal flips to Authorized. Screens missing from Figma — steps 4–5, the pre-check, the IP owner review queue — are filled in. Data stays in your browser.
07商业模式与路线图Business model & roadmap收入来自三部分：交易佣金、IP 方的赞助活动与广告费，以及面向 IP 方的 B2B 订阅（需求数据与审核工具）。主要成本集中在产品研发、IP 审核与法律合规、社区运营、IP 方拓展，以及云服务、支付与客服。路线图分五个阶段：创作者提交与 IP 匹配 MVP → 社区验证系统 → IP 方审核后台 → 授权生产与销售上线 → 可扩展的 IP 周边生态。Revenue comes from three streams: a commission on each sale, sponsored IP campaigns and advertising, and a B2B subscription that gives IP owners demand data and review tools. The main costs are product and engineering, IP review and legal compliance, community growth, IP-owner acquisition, and cloud, payment and support operations. The roadmap has five phases: a creator submission and IP-matching MVP → a community validation system → an IP owner approval portal → licensed production and sales → a scalable IP merch ecosystem.

IPLY 是一个连接粉丝创作者与 IP 方的周边共创与授权平台。粉丝提交周边提案，由社区投票与预售验证需求，再交给 IP 方审核授权并生产上架，探索如何让粉丝创意以合规、可持续的方式变成官方商品。IPLY is a co-creation and licensing platform connecting fan creators with IP owners. Fans submit merch proposals, the community validates demand through votes and pre-orders, and IP owners approve and license them for production — exploring how fan creativity can become official products in a legitimate, sustainable way.
