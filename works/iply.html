IPLY 是一个连接粉丝创作者与 IP 方的周边共创与授权平台。粉丝提交周边提案，由社区投票与预售验证需求，再交给 IP 方审核授权并生产上架，探索如何让粉丝创意以合规、可持续的方式变成官方商品。IPLY is a co-creation and licensing platform connecting fan creators with IP owners. Fans submit merch proposals, the community validates demand through votes and pre-orders, and IP owners approve and license them for production — exploring how fan creativity can become official products in a legitimate, sustainable way.
