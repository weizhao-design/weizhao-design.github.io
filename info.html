关于About

我是一名交互设计师，毕业于 SAVANNAH COLLEGE OF ART AND DESIGN 交互设计与游戏开发硕士项目（2026 年 11 月），本科就读于北京服装学院视觉传达设计专业。I'm an interaction designer graduating from the MA in Interactive Design and Game Development at Savannah College of Art and Design (Nov 2026), with a BFA in Visual Communication from Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology.

我的创作关注 AI 体验、数字产品、PHYSICAL COMPUTING 与未来导向设计，探索技术如何更自然地融入日常生活，并在数字系统与现实空间之间建立更有温度的连接。My work focuses on AI experiences, digital products, physical computing and future-oriented design — exploring how technology can fit more naturally into everyday life and build warmer connections between digital systems and physical space.

我擅长从用户研究、问题定义、交互流程、原型制作到视觉界面进行完整设计推进，也关注 AI 与新兴技术在真实场景中的体验落地。I carry projects from user research and problem framing through interaction flows and prototyping to visual interfaces, with a focus on bringing AI and emerging technology into real-world use.
